## High-Level Conclusion

- `DSC v5` improves over `SkillNet baseline` on all three datasets.
- `DSC v5` also improves over `old DSC` on all three datasets.
- The strongest gains are on:
  - `ScienceWorld`: higher reward while keeping `100%` success and reducing steps.
  - `ALFWorld`: higher reward/success and clearly shorter trajectories.
  - `WebShop`: still the hardest environment, but v5 finally beats both baseline and old DSC in reward, success rate, and step efficiency.

## Remaining Behavioral Issues Observed in WebShop

- Some solved-looking trajectories still fail because the agent stays on the detail page and outputs `Action: none` instead of finishing with `click[Buy Now]`.
- Some no-solution trajectories degenerate into repeated natural-language “no match found” outputs, which can trigger runtime recompile or waste remaining steps.
- These suggest the next useful improvements are:
  - stronger finish-action enforcement on satisfiable detail pages
  - a guard that converts non-executable natural-language outputs into explicit recovery actions
